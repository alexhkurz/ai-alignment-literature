## Summary

Philosophical / architectural commentary on Anthropic’s J-space / global-workspace findings in Claude. Frames three open questions left by the Jacobian-lens results (around persistence, subjecthood, and closed dynamics) and proposes “reentry”—closed, protected recurrent loops in the authors’ related mathematical models of subjecthood—as an architectural fix for what feedforward residual streams leave unresolved. Positions reentry neural systems as a candidate basis for intrinsic safety and subject-like continuity beyond episode-bounded workspaces.

## Key concepts

- Three unanswered questions about J-space after Gurnee et al.
- Reentry as closed protected recurrence for persistence / subjecthood
- Critique of purely feedforward workspace realizations
- Link to authors’ Φ-analogue / reentry-system program
