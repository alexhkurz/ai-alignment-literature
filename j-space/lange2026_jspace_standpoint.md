## Summary

Applies Lange’s Standpoint Threshold (Philosophies 2026)—three jointly necessary architectural conditions: persistent internal context, differentiated internal possibility, and closed-loop operativity—to Gurnee et al.’s J-space results. Condition 1 fails for the episode-bounded Claude process studied (workspace contents are reconstructed from supplied context rather than carried across the relevant boundary). Causal interventions support Condition 2’s differentiation sub-diagnostic without establishing autonomous stance maintenance; selectivity experiments strongly support Condition 3’s role differentiation. Conclusion: J-space upgrades the evidential status of a bounded near-case but does not change the negative threshold classification. Proposes three tests (Input Equalization, Continuation-Reconstruction, Active-Carryover) for persistent architectures.

## Key concepts

- Standpoint Threshold Conditions 1–3 applied to J-space
- Reconstructive vs maintained internal orientation
- Bounded near-case (not a positive consciousness claim)
- Input Equalization / Continuation-Reconstruction / Active-Carryover tests
