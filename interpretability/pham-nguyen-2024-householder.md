## Summary

Proposes Householder Pseudo-Rotation (HPR), an activation-editing method that treats activations as having both direction and magnitude. Unlike standard steering vectors, HPR applies a rotation-like transformation via Householder reflections, preserving activation norms and improving consistency. The method is evaluated on safety benchmarks and shows stronger performance than simple additive steering while maintaining the original model's overall capabilities.

## Key concepts

- **Householder Pseudo-Rotation (HPR)** — rotation-based activation transformation.  
- **Direction-magnitude perspective** — decouple and preserve activation norm.  
- **Activation editing** — alter internal representations at inference time.  
- **Safety benchmarks** — evaluate harm reduction without degrading general abilities.
