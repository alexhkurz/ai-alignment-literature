## Summary

Defines **representation engineering (RepE)**: monitor and manipulate high-level cognitive variables in DNNs by reading/writing representations rather than individual neurons. Demonstrates RepE baselines for honesty, harmlessness, power-seeking, and related safety-relevant traits in LLMs via reading vectors and control vectors applied at inference time—positioning top-down transparency as complementary to bottom-up circuit analysis.

## Key concepts

- **Representation engineering (RepE)** — top-down transparency via representations.  
- **Reading / control vectors** — extract and steer latent concepts.  
- **Safety-relevant monitoring** — honesty, harm, power-seeking probes.
