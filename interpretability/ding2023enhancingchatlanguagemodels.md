## Summary

Introduces UltraChat, a 1.5-million-turn instructional conversation dataset generated without human queries, and UltraLLaMA, a LLaMA model fine-tuned on it. The dataset is designed for diversity, scale, and topic breadth across multi-turn dialogues. Evaluated against Dolly, OpenAssistant, Alpaca, and Vicuna, the resulting model scores strongly on ChatGPT-based evaluation, supporting the hypothesis that scaling high-quality instruction data improves open-source chat model performance.

## Key concepts

- **UltraChat** — 1.5M multi-turn instruction conversations generated at scale.  
- **Instruction-tuning data** — scaling data diversity/quality as the driver of chat-model capability.  
- **UltraLLaMA** — LLaMA fine-tuned on UltraChat, benchmarked against Vicuna/Alpaca.  
- **ChatGPT-based evaluation** — scoring open assistant responses with another LLM.
