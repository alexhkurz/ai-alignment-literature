## Summary

Proposes controlling the reasoning strategies of large reasoning models (LRMs) — such as backtracking, cross-verification, procedural planning, and multi-perspective reasoning — by steering hidden states through sparse autoencoders. Existing strategy control is limited because different reasoning strategies are entangled in LRM hidden representations. SAE-based steering decomposes the representation, allowing targeted intervention in the model's reasoning process to correct or redirect individual steps.

## Key concepts

- **Reasoning strategy control** — steering which cognitive strategy (backtracking, planning, verification) the model uses.  
- **Sparse autoencoder-based steering** — SAEs disentangle strategy-entangled hidden states.  
- **LRM reasoning traces** — explicit step-by-step generation in reasoning models.  
- **Controllable step-level intervention** — correcting or redirecting the reasoning path at a flaw.
