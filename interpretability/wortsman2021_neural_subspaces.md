## Summary

Observes that neural loss landscapes contain paths of diverse high-accuracy solutions and wide minima. Learns lines, curves, and simplexes of networks in a **single training run** at cost comparable to one model—parameterizing endpoints and optimizing along the connecting subspace. Subspace midpoints improve accuracy, calibration, and label-noise robustness vs. standard training and SWA; diverse subspace points ensemble without multiple independent runs.

## Key concepts

- **Neural network subspaces** — manifolds of solutions in weight space.  
- **Single-run ensembling** — diversity without retraining cost.  
- **Subspace midpoint** — improved generalization and calibration.
