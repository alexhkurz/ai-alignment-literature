## Summary

Investigates sparse autoencoders (SAEs) on synthetic formal-language testbeds (Dyck-2, Expr, English PCFG) to better understand what SAEs can and cannot recover from language model hidden states. The authors find that interpretable latents can emerge under some hyperparameter settings, but performance is highly sensitive to training-pipeline inductive biases. Correlation between latent activity and input features does not always imply causality, mirroring limitations previously observed in vision.

## Key concepts

- **SAEs on formal languages** — controlled testbeds for studying feature recovery.  
- **Dyck-2, Expr, English PCFG** — synthetic language tasks with known structure.  
- **Causality vs. correlation** — latents can correlate with features without causing them.  
- **Hyperparameter sensitivity** — SAE success depends heavily on training choices.
