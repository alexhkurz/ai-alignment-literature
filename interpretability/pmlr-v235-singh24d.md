# Representation Surgery: Theory and Practice of Affine Steering

## Summary

Provides a formal and empirical study of affine steering functions for language model representations. The authors derive two least-squares-optimal affine steering transformations under different constraints, justify existing steering approaches, and propose a new improved steering method. Experiments demonstrate that affine steering can reduce toxic and biased generation without significant loss in output quality.

## Key concepts

- **Affine steering functions** — linear/affine transformations of hidden representations.  
- **Least-squares optimal steering** — derive theoretically best steering under constraints.  
- **Toxicity and bias mitigation** — shift representations to reduce undesirable outputs.  
- **Representation surgery** — edit the internal encoding of a target behavior.
