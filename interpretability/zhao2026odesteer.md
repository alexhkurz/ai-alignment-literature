## Summary

Frames activation steering in an ODE-based control-theory formalism: standard activation addition is a first-order Euler step of an underlying ODE, and steering directions correspond to barrier functions from control theory. **ODESteer** uses log-density-ratio barrier functions to define multi-step ODE steering, improving alignment vs. one-shot activation addition on empirical benchmarks.

## Key concepts

- **ODE perspective on steering** — activation addition as discretized flow.  
- **Barrier functions** — control-theoretic steering direction design.  
- **Multi-step steering** — beyond single forward-pass vector add.
