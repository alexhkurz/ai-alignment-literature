## Summary

Systematic study of steering vectors (SVs) for LLM behavior control. Shows substantial limitations in- and out-of-distribution: steerability varies widely across inputs, spurious dataset biases can dominate apparent steering success, and OOD generalization is weak. Argues for caution when deploying SV methods as broadly reliable alignment tools.

## Key concepts

- **Steering vectors** — activation-space intervention directions.  
- **Reliability / generalization** — input-dependent effectiveness.  
- **Spurious correlations** — steering success confounded by dataset structure.
