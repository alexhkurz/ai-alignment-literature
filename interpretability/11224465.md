## Summary

Human-subjects study of *style vectors* (activation-engineering steering directions) for controllable text generation in LLMs. Evaluates whether steering along learned style directions reliably shifts emotional tone and related stylistic properties as intended, comparing automated metrics with human judgments of adherence and quality.

## Key concepts

- **Style vectors** — activation directions for emotion/style control.  
- **Human evaluation** — subjective assessment of steering effectiveness.  
- **Controllable generation** — inference-time activation interventions.
