## Summary

Survey/tutorial of **analysis methods** for neural NLP: probing, representation similarity, attention analysis, and caveats (selectivity, confounds, causality vs correlation).

## Key concepts

- **Probing taxonomy** — what probes can and cannot show.  
- **Representational analysis** — comparing layers and models.  
- **Methodological pitfalls** — control tasks, random baselines.
