## Summary

Proposes LayerNavigator to identify which transformer layers are most effective for activation steering, reducing the search cost of layer selection. Ranks layers by predicted intervention benefit so practitioners can steer at a subset of layers without exhaustive trial-and-error across the full stack.

## Key concepts

- **Layer selection for steering** — not all layers contribute equally.  
- **Efficient search** — navigate layer space for intervention points.  
- **Activation steering** — inference-time residual-stream edits.
