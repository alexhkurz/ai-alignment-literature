## Summary

Proposes Feature Guided Activation Additions (FGAA), a steering method that combines ideas from Contrastive Activation Addition and SAE-targeted steering. FGAA operates in the sparse-autoencoder latent space, selecting interpretable SAE features through optimization to construct human-understandable steering vectors. Experiments on Gemma-2-2B and Gemma-2-9B show that FGAA achieves stronger steering effects while keeping the steered outputs coherent.

## Key concepts

- **Feature Guided Activation Additions (FGAA)** — SAE-latent steering with interpretable features.  
- **Contrastive Activation Addition + SAE-TS** — combine two prior steering ideas.  
- **Human-interpretable steering vectors** — choose features that humans can inspect.  
- **Coherence-quality trade-off** — maintain output fluency under strong steering.
