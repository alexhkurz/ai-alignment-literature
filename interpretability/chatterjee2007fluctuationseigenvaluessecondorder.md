## Summary

Develops a unified soft-toolkit for central limit theorems on linear statistics of random-matrix eigenvalues. Introduces *second-order Poincaré inequalities*: whereas ordinary Poincaré bounds control variance of smooth functions of independent variables, the second-order version links fluctuations of \(g(X)\) to the typical size of the Hessian \(\nabla^2 g(X)\), yielding normal approximation via Stein's method. HeRA cites a corollary bounding the Frobenius norm of a covariance matrix by the expected squared Frobenius norm of the Jacobian.

## Key concepts

- **Second-order Poincaré inequality** — CLT-style control from Hessian smallness.  
- **Linear eigenvalue statistics** — sums \(\sum_i f(\lambda_i)\) without \(\sqrt{n}\) normalization.  
- **Stein's method** — normal approximation for smooth functions of independent Gaussians.
