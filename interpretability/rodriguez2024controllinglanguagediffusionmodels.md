## Summary

Introduces Activation Transport (ACT), an activation-steering framework grounded in optimal transport theory. ACT is modality-agnostic and can be applied to both large language models and text-to-image diffusion models with negligible computational overhead. For LLMs, it reduces toxicity, induces target concepts, and increases truthfulness; for diffusion models, it enables fine-grained style control and concept negation. The method is shown to generalize and unify many prior activation-steering approaches.

## Key concepts

- **Activation Transport (ACT)** — steer activations via optimal transport.  
- **Modality-agnostic control** — LLMs and text-to-image diffusion models.  
- **Optimal transport theory** — formal justification for transporting representations.  
- **Fine-grained concept control** — style, toxicity, and truthfulness steering.
