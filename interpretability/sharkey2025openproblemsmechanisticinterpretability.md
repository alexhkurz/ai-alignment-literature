## Summary

Surveys open problems in mechanistic interpretability, covering both conceptual and technical challenges. The authors organize the field around what it would take to reliably understand and predict model behavior through circuits, features, and causal interventions. The paper identifies key bottlenecks such as scaling interpretability to large models, validating causal claims, and translating mechanistic insights into safety guarantees.

## Key concepts

- **Mechanistic interpretability open problems** — a community-wide research agenda.  
- **Circuit tracing and features** — core primitives for reverse-engineering networks.  
- **Causal validation** — moving from correlation to mechanistic understanding.  
- **Safety relevance** — using interpretability to detect and prevent harmful behavior.
