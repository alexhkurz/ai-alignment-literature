## Summary

Seth presents a predictive-processing account of consciousness in which perception is controlled hallucination and the self is a dynamically maintained model rather than a fixed inner essence. The book distinguishes levels of consciousness, contents of consciousness, and forms of selfhood, and emphasizes embodiment and interoception as central to conscious experience. A major theme is explanatory progress without invoking non-natural dualist assumptions.

## Authors

Anil Seth is a neuroscientist at the University of Sussex and one of the leading contemporary researchers on consciousness science, especially predictive and embodied approaches.


## Key concepts

- **Controlled hallucination** - perception as prediction constrained by sensory input.
- **Predictive self-model** - selfhood as an ongoing inferential construction.
- **Embodied consciousness** - bodily regulation and interoception are foundational.
- **Explanatory naturalism** - consciousness explained within biological/cognitive science.
