## Summary

This talk walks through the Anthropic *Global Workspace* paper (Gurnee, Sofroniew, Pearce, et al., 2026). It focuses on the **J-Lens** / **J-Space** technique for identifying internal representations that a model can verbalize, and on the five proposed workspace-like properties: verbal report, direct modulation, internal reasoning, flexible generalization, and selectivity. The slides cover how to read from and write to the J-Lens, how to swap concepts in J-Space, and experimental evidence that ablating J-Space affects meta-language and multi-step reasoning tasks more than automatic processes. The last section compares the findings against several theories of consciousness (GWT, higher-order theories, attention schema, recurrent processing, IIT).
