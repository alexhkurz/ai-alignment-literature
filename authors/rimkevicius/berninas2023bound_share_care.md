## Summary

Cross-cultural evidence on how people relate teleological forces (fate, gods) and non-teleological ones (luck, chance) to everyday choice and to prosocial obligation ('bound to share' vs 'not to care').

## Key concepts

- **fate**
- **gods**
- **luck**
- **chance**
- **cross-cultural prosociality**
