# Being prosocial and happy, and believing in life-determining forces across cultures

**Authors:** R Berniūnas, P Rimkevičius, A Beinorius, V Dranseika, V Silius  
**Year:** 2024  
**Venue:** Journal of Cultural Cognitive Science  
**Citation key:** `berninas2024prosocial_happy`  
**PDF / link:** [https://doi.org/10.1007/s41809-023-00139-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41809-023-00139-x)  
**Google Scholar:** [Search](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Being%20prosocial%20and%20happy%2C%20and%20believing%20in%20life-determining%20forces%20across%20cultures)

## Summary

Cross-cultural study linking beliefs about life-determining forces (fate, gods, luck, chance, free will) to prosocial behaviour and happiness. Effects of internal vs external force beliefs vary by culture and by the type of force.
