# The weirdness of belief in free will

**Authors:** R Berniūnas, A Beinorius, V Dranseika, V Silius, P Rimkevičius  
**Year:** 2021  
**Venue:** Consciousness and Cognition  
**Citation key:** `berninas2021weirdness_free_will`  
**PDF / link:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2020.103054](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2020.103054)  
**Google Scholar:** [Search](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The%20weirdness%20of%20belief%20in%20free%20will)

## Summary

Cross-cultural and conceptual argument that belief in free will is 'weird' relative to folk metaphysics in many traditions — not a universal default — with implications for x-phi and moral responsibility debates.

## Key concepts

- **free will belief**
- **cross-cultural weirdness**
- **experimental philosophy**
- **moral responsibility**
