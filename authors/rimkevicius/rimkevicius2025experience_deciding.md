## Summary

EEG study of what agents report being aware of at the moment of deciding. Pre-movement neural signals correlate with reported clarity and vividness of deciding for actual movements, and with felt activity of imagining for imagined movements — suggesting agents are at least partly aware of influences often treated as unconscious in Libet-style paradigms.
