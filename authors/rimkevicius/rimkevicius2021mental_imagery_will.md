# Mental imagery and the illusion of conscious will

**Authors:** Paulius Rimkevičius  
**Year:** 2021  
**Venue:** Synthese  
**Citation key:** `rimkevicius2021mental_imagery_will`  
**PDF / link:** [https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-020-02992-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-020-02992-7)  
**Google Scholar:** [Search](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Mental%20imagery%20and%20the%20illusion%20of%20conscious%20will)

## Summary

Argues that vivid mental imagery can contribute to the phenomenology of conscious will and may help explain illusions of authorship in thought and action, connecting imagery research to Wegner-style debates on conscious will.

- **mental imagery**
- **conscious will**
