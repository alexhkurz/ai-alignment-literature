# The Interpretive-Sensory Access Theory of Self-Knowledge: Simplicity and Coherence with Surrounding Theories

**Year:** 2019  
**Venue:** Problemos  
**Citation key:** `rimkevicius2019interpretive_sensory_simplicity`

## Summary

Argues the ISA theory of self-knowledge is the simplest option among alternatives when judged against surrounding theories (transparency views, predictive processing). Replies to objections from unified transparency and PP frameworks.

- **ISA theory**
